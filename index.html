About Me

My name is Vlad, and I work as a freelance frontend developer. I build clean and responsive websites for personal brands, small businesses, services, and independent projects.

I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress, GitHub, Vercel, and Figma-to-code workflows. I care about structure, readability, and thoughtful visual presentation, so the final result feels not only functional, but also polished and reliable.

I prefer simple, well-organized solutions over unnecessary complexity. A good website should look modern, work smoothly on different devices, and communicate clearly from the first screen.
