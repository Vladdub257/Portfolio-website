About Me

My name is Vlad, and I build clean, responsive websites for small businesses, specialists, personal brands, and independent projects.

I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, WordPress, GitHub, Vercel, and Figma-to-code workflows. My focus is clear structure, balanced layout, and frontend work that feels polished, reliable, and easy to use.

I prefer simple, well-organized solutions over unnecessary complexity. A good website should communicate clearly, work smoothly on different devices, and leave a strong first impression.

How the Work Process Looks

I prefer a simple and transparent workflow. Clear steps help avoid confusion, save time, and make the result more predictable for both sides. Most small projects are completed within a few days to a couple of weeks, depending on scope.

01

Discuss the Task

We define the goal, structure, and what the website needs to communicate.

02

Estimate Price and Timeline

I review the scope and provide a clear price range and realistic delivery estimate.

03

Build the First Version

I create the first working version with responsive layout, visual clarity, and clean structure.

04

Revisions

We review the result and make agreed improvements to refine details and better match the goal.

05

Final Delivery

You receive the finished website or code files, ready for use, editing, or deployment.
